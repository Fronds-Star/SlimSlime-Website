Oobleck Slime

From the word oobleck means a substance that acts like a liquid, and can be poured, but that acts like a solid when you apply force to it by pushing it or squeezing it. So we will make a slime that is an oobleck!

Ingredients

  • 2 c cornstarch
  • 1.5 c water
  • Food coloring or acrylic paint (optional)

How Long Does This Slime Last?

This kind of slime can last just a few hours by drying up the water. You can play it again by adding a few mL of water.

If you want a bigger batch – multiply the recipe below.

Procedure

  • Pour cornstarch in a mixing bowl.
  • Add 1.5 c water into the bowl.
  • Add your food coloring until you are satisfied with the color.
  • Mix until the mixture is evenly distributed.

ENJOY!


So in this kind of slime, your hand sinks as you relax on your oobleck slime. And if you apply force with your hand on your oobleck slime, your hand bounces instead of sinking.

Making a oobleck slime isn't hard to look at. Those two basic materials, water and cornstarch are just available in your home to have fun with an oobleck slime!
