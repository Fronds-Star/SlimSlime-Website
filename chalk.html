Chalkboard Slime

What’s even better than slime? Slime that you can use as a chalkboard, of course!

Ingredients

  • White Glue
  • Baking Soda
  • Contact Lens Solution (must contain boric acid)
  • Chalkboard Paint (purchase at craft store)
  • Chalk Markers

Slime Recipe

You can mark, write and make drawing in this kind of slime! And it is very creative as you know.

Procedure

  • Pour 4 oz of glue into a mixing bowl.
  • Add 2 Tablespoons of chalkboard paint to the glue and mix thoroughly.
  • Add 1 teaspoon of baking soda to the glue/paint and stir to combine.
  • Add ½ teaspoon of contact lens solution and stir. The slime should begin to thicken. (Use a sturdy spoon for this! Plastic ones can break.)
  • If the slime is still sticky, add in a few drops of contact lens solution at a time and stir until the slime begins to pull away form the sides of the bowl.
  • Take the slime out of the bowl and knead it for a minute or two to fully combine all ingredients.
  • After making your slime you can draw on it!
  • Using chalkboard markers, you can draw on the slime by pressing down to make dots.
  • When you are done with your design distort it! Pull the slime and watch your drawing elongate.
  • To make a new drawing simply knead the slime until the color disappears and start again.

ENJOY!


DO NOT add too much contact lens solution! We did and your slime will turn into a rubber like substance. On second thought that also is a good science experiment. Store your slime in an airtight baggie. Note that this slime will be more rubbery as it sits over time.

Don’t draw on the slime like you would on paper. The marker tip will get stuck in the slime. Mark the slime using dots instead.
