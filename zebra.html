Glitter Glue VS. Clear Glue

As you may know, clear slime is applicable for ordinary and for jiggly slime. When kneading the slime, your clear slime forms foggy texture inside of you slime and that foggy texture are bubbles! So if you want to return your slime in a transparent color, store your slime in a container in 8 or more hours.
