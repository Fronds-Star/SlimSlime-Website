Iceberg Slime

Iceberg slime has a properties of being crispy of the surface area of your slime and the interior of the slime is soft as the fluffy slime.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups white glue
  • 2-5 mL water
  • 4 pumps liquid soap
  • 10 cups shaving foam - note: do not use shave gel.
  • Food coloring or acrylic paint (optional)
  • Slime Activator, click here to make slime activator

How Long Does This Slime Last?

Iceberg slime is just a fluffy slime with a little differences so the fluffines of this slime can last a day. But you can still play with it but but it will turn into a consistency more like regular slime.

If you want a bigger batch – multiply the recipe below.

Procedure

  • Pour glue in a mixing bowl.
  • Add about 4 pumps of lotion.
  • Add 10 cups of shaving foam.
  • Add your food coloring until you are satisfied with the color.
  • Mix until it becomes a homogeneous mixture.
  • Add slime activiator 1 tablespoon at a time until the slime forms.
  • Knead the slime until it lessen its stickiness.
  • Smoothen the top layer or the surface area of the slime.
  • Store the slime in the refrigerator for at least 1.5 day.
  • Do not cover your slime with a lid.

ENJOY!


Iceberg slime is just a fluffy slime with a crispy top layer surface. It is satisfying at first breaking the top layer surface, sinking it with the soft part of the slime!
