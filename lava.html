Lava Slime

This slime is perfect for creating a volcanic landscape for toy dinosaurs!

Ingredients

  • White Fluffy Slime or Jiggly Slime
  • Yellow, Red, and Orange coloring
  • Red glitter

Slime Recipe

This lava slime is easy to make and you can make a scenes with it and you can add toys and legos to make it more appealing to the eyes.

Procedure

  • Prepare your white chosen slime and the other ingredients.
  • Apply the colors to your slime. It will be more appealing if you make a unicorn slime out of those colors.
  • Add some glitter onto your slime.
  • Knead the slime to form like a lava slime.
  • Add some toys and legos for more appealing. This is optiona.

ENJOY!


By this point, most of the slime was mixed together. This resulted in a beautiful, sparkly dark orange slime. We’re still playing with the lava slime now! Snoopy and Little Hurricane have enjoyed exploring it as well. For those kiddos who aren’t into Lego Star Wars, dinosaurs are a great alternative for molten lava slime pretend play!
