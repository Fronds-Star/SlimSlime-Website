Apple Sparkle Slime

Sparkly apple slime perfect for an apple theme, or just because! Love that you can make it scented, too. Apple slime is quick, easy, and low cost to make and play with. It can get messy so play if you dare.

Clear Slime

  • Prepare the ingredients for making red glitter slime
  • Mix all the ingredients without the slime activator.
  • Add few drops of apple scented soap
  • Add slime activator to your slime
  • Knead your slime until it is not sticky.

ENJOY!

Apple Sparkle Slime

This slime has the potential to be apple scented. That is where the apple soap comes in. I added a little apple scented soap to the inside of the apples before placing them on the trays. By adding the slime to the apple it mixed with the soap. Then making the slime smell like apples.
