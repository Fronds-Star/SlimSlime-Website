Ocean Slime

Swirling ocean waves of color! Make ocean slime for an AWESOME under the sea theme this summer. Use any of our basic slimes including saline solution slime recipe, borax slime recipe, and liquid starch slime recipe to make this ocean theme slime recipe the kids will love to play with!

Ocean Slime

  • Prepare a jiggly slime that uses clear glue.
  • If you want your slime more jiggly, add more water.
  • Add few drops of blue coloring food if you want your slime colored and translucent.
  • Add slime activator to your slime
  • Knead your slime until it is not sticky.
  • Store your clear slime in a ziplock for 8 hours.

ENJOY!

STORING YOUR OCEAN SLIME

Slime lasts quite awhile! I get a lot of questions regarding how I store my slime. We use reusable containers in either plastic or glass. Make sure to keep your slime clean and it will last for several weeks.
