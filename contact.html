Contact Lens Solution

In this tutorial, we will learn how to make a contact lens solution with baking soda step by step with pictures.

Ingredients







  • 1 1/2 tbsp contact lens NOTE: Must have boric acid and sodium borate otherwise it wouldn't work.
  • 1/2 tsp baking soda
  • your slime mixture

Safety Tips: Adults only should make the slime and handle all chemicals. Do not substitute any ingredients in our slime recipes. If you have sensitive skin, wear gloves while playing with the slime. Always wash hands before and after use. If you notice any skin irritation discontinue use immediately. See the bottom of the post for more safety precautions.

Procedure


  • Prepare your slime mixture that is ready for slime activator.
  • Apply 1 1/2 tbsp contact lens solution. Add more if needed.
  • Mix well until it is well combined.
  • Add 1/2 tsp baking powder. Add more until slime is form
