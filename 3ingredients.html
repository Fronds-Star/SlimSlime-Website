Slime Ingredents

As you may know, there are many optional ingredients and main ingredients to make a right choice of your slime. So lets learn every purpose of each slime ingredients.

•Cornstarch


Cornstarch is the substitution of baby powder. Baby powder and corstarch is optional so it does not need when making a slime and they both can give a matte finish on your slime. If you add less cornstarch, you will get a more liquid consistency. Adding more cornstarch will make for a harder slime.

•Baby Oil


Baby oil is optional so it does not need when making a slime. Baby oil is used when you're making a glossy slime or glazed slime because the main purpose of baby oil in your slime is for glossiness.

•Foaming Face Wash


Foaming face wash is commonly used in fluffy slime. So it is like a substitution of shaving foam. Common brand of foaming face wash is Clean and Clear. The purpose of foaming face wash is for making your slime fluffier but it will need a tool to make the foaming face wash fluffy. Electric mixer is the best tool to make your slime fluffy using foaming face wash.

•Liquid Soap


Liquid soap is optional so it does not need when making a slime. Liquid soap can only helps the slime activator in activating slime or forming slime.

•Conditioner


Conditioner is optional so it does not need when making a slime. The purpose of conditioner is just like lotion, for making your slime stretchy and it can make your slime slossy a little bit.
