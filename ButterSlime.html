Butter Slime

Butter slime is soft as the fluffy slime and butter slime has the ability to stretch as wide as a meter! It is the most strechy of all slimes and that is because of one ingredient, the Daiso Clay.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups white glue
  • 5-10 mL water
  • 10 pumps liquid soap
  • Daiso soft clay
  • Food coloring or acrylic paint (optional)
  • Slime Activator, click here to make slime activator

How Long Does This Slime Last?

Butter slime can last just a few weeks! Most slimes can last just a few weeks. Don't forget to store your butter slime in a room temperature!

If you want a bigger batch – multiply the recipe below.

Procedure

  • Pour glue in a mixing bowl.
  • Add about 10 pumps of lotion.
  • Add your food coloring until you are satisfied with the color.
  • Mix until it becomes a homogeneous mixture.
  • Add slime activiator 1 tablespoon at a time until the slime forms.
  • Knead the slime until it lessen its stickiness.
  • Add daiso clay into the slime.
  • Mix the clay and the slime until it becomes a clay-like slime.
  • Apply about 2 pumps of lotion into the slime.

ENJOY!


Butter slime is literally not a butter. It is called a butter slime because of its characteristics and its ability to stetch widely.

This kind of slime has the characteristics of a clay too! That's because one of its ingredients is a soft clay. I recommend to use soft clay from daiso, japanese store than a ordinary clay.
