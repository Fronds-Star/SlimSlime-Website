Snow Slime

While this icy cold snow slime would make sense during the winter, I think it would be perfect in the heat of the summer. A great way to cool down when the temperatures are out of control.

Ingredients

  • White Fluffy Slime or Jiggly Slime
  • Refrigerator
  • Silver glitter

Quick Tip

Make your snow slime COLD by placing the ingredients the refrigerator for an hour before play. And the the viscosity of the slime is high so it means that the amount of slime activator applied in your slime is small.

Procedure

  • Put your white chosen slime in refrigerator for an hour.
  • Apply glitter on your slime
  • Add some slime activator if the viscosity of the slime is still high.
  • Now touch your slime when summer comes!

ENJOY!


DO NOT add too much contact lens solution! We did and your slime will turn into a rubber like substance. On second thought that also is a good science experiment. Store your slime in an airtight baggie. Note that this slime will be more rubbery as it sits over time.

If you want a frozen slime, store your slime in a freezer for an hour. Then, give it to kids, and they will marvel as it melts in their hands! Store your slime in an airtight container between play times, and it can be used again and again!
